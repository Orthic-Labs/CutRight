**Visual rules from brand book:**
- Logo never altered, rotated, or recolored outside palette
- Min size: 3cm wide for full lockup, 2cm for icon
- Clear space = height of letter U around logo
- Logo can't be placed on textured backgrounds (other than the brand pattern itself)
- Forbidden: pink/purple bg, broken-up arrangements, off-palette colors

**Voice rules (locked from live site copy):**
- Headlines: ALL CAPS, condensed, fragment-style. Two-beat pattern: setup + punchline. ("NOT FAST FASHION. THE ANTIDOTE." / "A DECADE INSIDE FAST FASHION. THIS IS THE EXIT.")
- Toxic green accent on the punchline word/phrase only — never on the setup
- Body copy: short, declarative, factual ("150 billion garments produced every year. 65% trashed within 12 months.")
- Founder voice (Adrian) speaks in first person from lived experience — never abstract
- Trust signals are specific: "Ships within 1-2 business days," "Ethically made in India · Ships from California," "Softest shirt or money back"
- Banned: vague sustainability speak ("eco-friendly," "conscious"), influencer slang, hype emoji, "limited drop"
- Use real numbers, real geography, real founder story

**Content restrictions**: Apply visual system + voice freely. NEVER fabricate manufacturing claims, fair-wage stats, or sustainability metrics — Adrian's actual supply chain only.
